Explore published cancer statistics

Understand treatment patterns with a clearer search.

Choose a cancer group and an age band or cancer stage. CasePath shows NHS-published statistics for that group, with counts, percentages, and source details.

What CasePath doesThis version filters a downloaded NHS statistics file. It does not use an AI model, identify individual patients, or recommend treatment.

What the results mean

They describe groups

The figures summarize tumour records with broad characteristics. They do not show individual patient journeys.

They do not pick treatment

Statistics can describe what was recorded. They cannot say which option is right for one person.

Your search stays here

Choices are used in this browser to filter the included NHS data file. They are not saved or sent by this page.

“Tumour resection” means surgery intended to remove the primary tumour. SACT means systemic anti-cancer treatment, including medicines such as chemotherapy and immunotherapy. “Other care” may include other types of treatment, care outside the measured period, private treatment, or missing records.

About

Kumaraswamy K.

MSc Health Data Science

My grandmother’s cancer journey inspired CasePath. As my family sought care, we spoke with several doctors and heard different treatment plans. Comparing the information was stressful, so I began wondering whether public cancer statistics could be easier to explore. CasePath is my way of making those treatment patterns clearer.
