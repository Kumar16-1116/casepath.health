Privacy notice

A plain-language explanation of what this prototype does with information.

Search choices

The cancer group, age band, stage, and treatment view you select are used in your browser to filter a bundled copy of published NHS group statistics. This prototype does not send or save those choices.

No patient records

The site does not ask for patient names, contact details, medical reports, or personal health records. The data included in the site contains aggregate statistics, not identifiable patient records.

Feedback

If you use the feedback form, it opens a draft email in your own email app. The website does not send it. Your email app will send the feedback only if you choose to press Send.

Hosting and external links

If the site is hosted online, the hosting provider may process standard technical connection information under its own privacy notice. Links to NHS and licence pages open external websites with their own privacy practices.

Questions

Contact kumaraswamyk1611@gmail.com with questions about this notice.
